## Current delivery boundary

The owner chose a labelled single-browser simulation for the immediate demo. Use fictional records and the Jaipur pedestrian-safety story. The Master PRD still requires a narrow, truthful assignment → observation/evidence → shared session/Youth Assembly → proposition with dissent → Workspace invitation/moderation → reflection/Passport path. Youth Parliament and external stakeholder routing may be clearly labelled prototypes. Browser-local state can demonstrate some of this sequence, but cannot establish shared participation, durable cross-device records, or server-side authorization. Label each simulated action and preserve those requirements as incomplete. The source also requires role separation, persistence, visible rulebooks, failure handling and a denied-access test for the connected path.

The reservation policy is approved in principle. Calculate `ceil(0.33 × applicable allocated places)` separately for each applicable allocation, with no pooling across activities, and leave unmet reserved places unfilled. Activity applicability under OD.19 and whether any documented exception is allowed under OD.17 remain open. Use fictional demo data only; real gender collection, access and retention under OD.18 remain undecided. Do not claim the rule is enforced until the relevant allocation flows, validation, rulebooks and tests exist. Public-launch legal/privacy and safeguarding gates remain separate from demo completion.

**Current-pass implementation rule:** package 02's production backend, shared session authority and secure permissions are deferred by the owner's single-browser choice, not completed. Packages 03–07 may demonstrate local, labelled interactions where feasible, but must not claim multi-user or server-backed behavior. Keep the connected requirement and its acceptance tests visible for a later delivery decision.

## Explicitly retained beyond this demo

The full product still needs server-backed multi-user governance, complete Parliamentary procedure, broader cohorts/competition and delegation workflows, stakeholder delivery, advanced Labs/Studio/analytics, provider integrations, Hindi content review, accessibility and safeguarding hardening, capacity/restore evidence, and legal/privacy/security launch reviews. The [scope guide](../../README_01_RULES_REQUIREMENTS_BY_SCOPE.md#23-scope-v--full-system-later-phase-backlog) and [reconciliation](../audits/MASTER_PRD_RECONCILIATION.md) remain the source map for these requirements. A public demo URL is not public-launch approval for real participant data.
