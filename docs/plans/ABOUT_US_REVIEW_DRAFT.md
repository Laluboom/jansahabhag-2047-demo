### About JanSahabhag 2047

Democracy depends on the understanding, judgement and participation of its citizens. JanSahabhag 2047 explores how education can help young people move from learning about society to taking part responsibly in it.

The initiative connects civic learning, careful observation, constructive dialogue and practical participation. It asks what could change if students learned not only about society, but also how to help shape it responsibly.

### The idea: LOK MANCH-2047

LOK MANCH-2047 is the civic participation concept behind the initiative. It envisions pathways between educational institutions, young citizens, democratic institutions and public representatives.

The aim is to help young people understand an issue, listen to different views, examine evidence and contribute constructively. This describes a concept under development, not an operating government platform or established institutional partnership.

### Our principles

1. **Understanding before action.** Observe carefully and learn the context.
2. **Dialogue before assumption.** Hear and examine different perspectives.
3. **Responsibility alongside rights.** Contribute with care for others and the public good.
4. **Education connected with life.** Relate learning to institutions and everyday responsibilities.
5. **Participation with purpose.** Value thoughtful contribution over presence alone.

### Looking towards 2047

As India approaches the centenary of its independence, JanSahabhag 2047 explores how education can nurture informed, responsible and participating citizens.
